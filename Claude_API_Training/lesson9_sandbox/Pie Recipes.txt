PIE RECIPES

1. Classic Apple Pie
Combine 6 sliced apples with sugar, cinnamon, and nutmeg. Pour into pie crust and bake at 375°F for 45-50 minutes until golden. Serve with vanilla ice cream.

2. Strawberry Shortcake Pie
Layer a buttery pie crust with whipped cream and fresh strawberries. Drizzle with strawberry jam. Chill for 2 hours before serving.

3. Chocolate Cream Pie
Fill pie crust with chocolate pudding, top with whipped cream and chocolate shavings. Refrigerate for 4 hours until set.

4. Key Lime Pie
Mix sweetened condensed milk, egg yolks, and key lime juice. Pour into crust and bake at 350°F for 15 minutes. Chill and top with whipped cream.

5. Peach Cobbler Pie
Layer sliced peaches with brown sugar and cinnamon in pie crust. Top with biscuit dough and bake at 375°F for 40 minutes.

6. Lemon Meringue Pie
Pour lemon custard filling into crust. Top with meringue and bake at 350°F for 10-12 minutes until golden brown.

7. Blueberry Pie
Combine fresh blueberries with sugar and lemon juice, pour into crust. Bake at 375°F for 45 minutes. Dust with powdered sugar.

8. Pumpkin Pie
Mix pumpkin puree, eggs, cream, and spices. Pour into crust and bake at 425°F for 15 minutes, then 350°F for 40 minutes.

9. Cherry Pie
Layer pitted cherries with sugar and cornstarch in crust. Top with lattice strips and bake at 375°F for 50 minutes.

10. Banana Cream Pie
Layer sliced bananas in custard-filled crust. Top generously with whipped cream and refrigerate for 3 hours before serving.
